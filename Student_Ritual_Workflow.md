# Student Manual: The 10-Step Daily Ritual & Review Workflow

Welcome to the core of your SKYLD learning experience! This guide will walk you through exactly how your daily tasks work, from starting your 10-step ritual to how you and your peers review each other's progress.

---

## Part 1: The 10-Step Daily Ritual

Every day, you are assigned a new vocabulary word. Your primary goal is to complete the 10-step ritual before midnight.

### How to Attempt Your Ritual
1. **Log in to your Dashboard:** You will see a large button for your "Daily Ritual." Click it to begin.
2. **Follow the Steps (1-9):** The application will guide you through learning the word. You will:
   - Read the definition and pronunciation.
   - Review synonyms and antonyms.
   - Write your own sentence using the word in context.
3. **Step 10: The Video Reflection (Upload):** 
   - After practicing the word, use a tool like **Loom** to record a 1-to-2 minute video of yourself speaking about the word and using it in context.
   - Paste the link to your video into the final submission box.
4. **Submit:** Click the submit button. 
   - *Reward:* You instantly earn **100 XP** and your Active Streak increases!

---

## Part 2: The Review System (Buddy, Peer, & Mentor)

Once you hit submit, the system automatically assigns your video to three different people for review to ensure you get the best feedback possible.

### 1. Buddy Review
You are paired with a specific "Study Buddy" in your cohort. You are responsible for keeping each other accountable.
- **When you submit:** Your buddy instantly receives an **In-App Notification** and an **Email** saying: *"Your buddy just submitted their 10-step ritual. Please review their submission!"*
- **Reviewing your Buddy:** Go to the **Review Queue** tab on your dashboard. Watch their video and provide constructive feedback.
- **Nudging your Buddy:** If it's getting late in the day and your buddy hasn't submitted their ritual yet, go to your **Study Buddy Monitor** tab. Click the **"Notify Buddy"** button. This sends an automatic email to their inbox telling them to get to work!

### 2. Cross-Pod Peer Review
To give you unbiased feedback, your video is also assigned to a random peer from a *different* team (Pod).
- **When assigned to you:** If the system selects *you* to review someone else's video, you will receive an email and in-app alert titled: **"🌐 Cross-Pod Review Assigned"**.
- Go to your Review Queue, watch the external student's video, and give them a fair score.

### 3. Mentor Evaluation
Finally, the Mentors assigned to your Pod will review your submission.
- **When you submit:** All Mentors in your Pod receive an email titled **"📋 Student Ritual Completed"**.
- Mentors use this to track your progress and provide your official scores for the Weekly Championship.

---

## Part 3: Understanding Notifications

To help you stay on track, the SKYLD platform uses a dual-notification system: **In-App Alerts** (the bell icon on your dashboard) and **Emails** sent directly to your inbox. 

Here is what you can expect to receive:

| Event | In-App Alert | Email Notification |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Buddy Nudge:** Your buddy clicks "Notify Buddy" to remind you to work. | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Buddy Review Assigned:** Your buddy finishes their ritual. | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Peer Review Assigned:** You are randomly selected to review an external peer. | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Admin Announcements:** Your Pod leader or Admin posts an important update. | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Deadline Reminder:** It's late in the day and you haven't finished your ritual yet. | ✅ | ✅ |

### Deadline Reminders
If you forget to do your ritual, don't worry! The system runs an automated check every day. If you haven't submitted your video by the evening, you will receive an email with a countdown (e.g., *"🔥 4h left — Complete your ritual"*), reminding you to save your daily streak before midnight.
