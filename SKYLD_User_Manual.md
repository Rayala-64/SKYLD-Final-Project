# SKYLD Application – Comprehensive User Manual

Welcome to **SKYLD**! This step-by-step guide is designed for complete beginners. Even if you have never used the platform before, this manual will show you exactly how to navigate your dashboard, complete your tasks, and interact with your peers and mentors.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Logging In
1. Go to the provided application URL (e.g., `https://skyld-pilot.netlify.app`).
2. Enter your registered Email and Password on the login screen.
3. If you forgot your password, click **"Forgot Password"**. You will receive an email with a link. Click it to create a new password.

---

## 2. Student Guide: Exploring Your Dashboard

When you log in as a student, you will land on your **Student Dashboard**. This is your main control center.

### 📊 Quick Stats (Top Cards)
At the top of your screen, you will see your current progress:
- **Active Streak:** Shows how many days in a row you have completed your daily rituals. Keep it going!
- **Total XP (Experience Points):** You earn XP by completing rituals and reviewing peers. This score helps you level up.
- **Batch Rank:** Shows your current ranking against all other students in your cohort.

### 🚀 Daily Rituals (The Most Important Button)
Right in the center, you will see a large button for your **Daily Mission** or **Daily Ritual**. 
- Click this button to start your 10-step vocabulary exercise for the day.
- **How to attempt it:** You will be given a specific word. Follow the 10 on-screen prompts (like writing meanings, synonyms, and creating sentences). 
- **The Final Step:** You will record a short 1-2 minute video (using Loom or similar) explaining the word. Paste the video link into the final box and hit **Submit**. Do this before midnight!

### 🗂️ Left Menu / Sidebar Navigation
On the left side of your screen, you have several tabs to explore:

1. **Learning Path / Word Vault:** 
   - Think of this as your personal dictionary. It stores all the words you have learned in the past. You can review them anytime.
2. **Review Queue:** 
   - This is where you go to check on your friends. If you are assigned to review a peer's video, it will appear here. Watch their video and give them a score and helpful feedback.
3. **Championships (Weekly Challenge):** 
   - Every week, your Pod (team) participates in a championship.
   - **How to upload:** Your Pod leader or team representative will record a final presentation video. Go to this tab to paste the video link and submit it on behalf of your team.
4. **My Pod Leaderboard:** 
   - See how you stack up against the other members of your specific team (Pod).
5. **Study Buddy Monitor:** 
   - You are paired with a specific Buddy. In this tab, you can see if they have completed their daily task. If they haven't, click the **"Notify Buddy"** button to send them a friendly email reminder to get to work!

## 3. Mentor Guide: Exploring Your Dashboard

Mentors are here to guide, review, and evaluate student progress. When you log in as a Mentor, your dashboard is tailored for evaluation.

### 👥 Assigned Students List
On your main dashboard, you will see a list of all the students assigned to your Pod.
- You can click on any student's name to view their individual progress, active streak, and history.

### ⭐ Evaluations Tab
This is your primary workspace.
- **Pending Reviews:** Whenever a student in your Pod completes their 10-step ritual and submits a video, it appears here. You will also receive an email notification.
- **How to Evaluate:** Click on the pending submission. Watch the student's video, read their text answers, and fill out the scoring rubric. Leave constructive feedback and hit submit. Your scores directly impact the student's XP and Batch Rank.

### 💬 My Pod Chat Interface
Communication is key to mentoring.
- Inside your dashboard, you have access to a **Pod Chat**. 
- Use this interface to send messages directly to your entire Pod. You can post tips, answer questions, or encourage them before the Weekly Championship.

---

## 4. Account Settings (For Everyone)

Whether you are a Student, Mentor, or Admin, you can always manage your account:
1. Click the **Gear Icon (Settings)** in the top navigation bar.
2. **Update Profile:** Change how your Full Name appears to others.
3. **Change Password:** If you know your current password but want a new one, use the "Security" section to reset it.
